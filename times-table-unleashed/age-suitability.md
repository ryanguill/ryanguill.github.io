# Times Table Unleashed Age Suitability

Times Table Unleashed is designed for children ages 6–8 who are building multiplication fluency.

The app provides age-appropriate, offline multiplication practice through short game rounds, hints, progress tracking, and a teacher report. It contains no advertising, no account creation, no in-app purchases, and no user-generated or social content.

The app does not collect data from children or other users. Learning progress is stored locally on the device.

The app includes a Parents & Teachers gate. Pressing and holding the gate for three seconds is required before the app opens its Privacy Policy or learning-research websites.
