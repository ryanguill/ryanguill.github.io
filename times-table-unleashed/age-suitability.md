# Times Table Unleashed Age Suitability

Times Table Unleashed is listed in the App Store Kids category for ages 6–8, helping families find an age-appropriate multiplication-practice app. Its learning experience is suitable for anyone who wants to build multiplication fluency, including older children, teens, and adults.

The app provides offline multiplication practice through short game rounds, hints, progress tracking, and a teacher report. It contains no advertising, no account creation, no in-app purchases, and no user-generated or social content.

The app does not collect data from children or other users. Learning progress is stored locally on the device.

The app includes a Parents & Teachers gate. Pressing and holding the gate for three seconds is required before the app opens its Privacy Policy or learning-research websites.
