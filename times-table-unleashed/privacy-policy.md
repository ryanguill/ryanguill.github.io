# Times Table Unleashed Privacy Policy

<p class="updated">Last updated: August 1, 2026</p>

<p class="summary">Times Table Unleashed does not create accounts, collect information on our servers, use advertising, use analytics, track users, or share information with third parties. Learning information stays on the device.</p>

## Information Stored on the Device

The app may store the following information locally on the device:

- An optional student nickname entered in the Teacher Report.
- Multiplication-practice results, including answers marked right or missed, mastery progress, streaks, scores, and completed mission summaries.
- Dates and times associated with progress records, so the app can show learning progress over time.

This information is used only to run the app’s learning features, personalize practice, and show the Mastery Map and Teacher Report.

## Information We Do Not Collect or Share

The current version of Times Table Unleashed does not:

- Require a child or parent account.
- Send the nickname or learning-progress information to us or to any third party.
- Use analytics, advertising, tracking, cookies, or third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
- Collect location, contacts, photos, videos, microphone recordings, camera data, email addresses, payment information, or device identifiers.
- Include in-app purchases, chat, social features, or user-generated content.

## Children’s Privacy

Times Table Unleashed is designed for elementary-age learners. We do not knowingly collect, transmit, or share children’s personal information. A nickname is optional, stays on the device, and is used only to label the local Teacher Report.

Parents and guardians should avoid entering a child’s full name or other identifying information as the nickname.

## Your Choices and Deleting Local Progress

A parent or guardian can erase the nickname and all learning progress from within the app by using **Erase This Device’s Progress** in the About area. Deleting the app also ordinarily removes its locally stored data from that device. Device backups are controlled by the device owner and Apple’s backup settings.

## External Links

Any links that open websites outside the app are placed behind a parental gate. External websites are governed by their own privacy policies.

## Changes to This Policy

If the app’s data practices change—for example, if we add accounts, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, purchases, or online features—we will update this policy before those changes are released.
